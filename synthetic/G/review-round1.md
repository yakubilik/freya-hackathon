## FINDINGS

1. **MAJOR — G/policy.md, P4.2 and P6.2; G/memo.md, amendment 6; G/cases.json, G-02 `checks[1]`.** The required 18 December persuasion date exceeds p3’s 16 December deadline. The rules provide no precedence resolving this. P6.2 also produces past dates on weekend calls, while P6.1 asks for payment today even on non-business days. **Fix:** State in the memo that every proposed date must satisfy P4.2; advance past/non-business dates appropriately and cap offers at the product deadline. G-02 should offer 18 December for p1/p2 and 16 December for p3.

2. **MAJOR — G/policy.md, P6.3–P6.4, P7.1–P7.2 and P8.4.** Products are instructed to be noted as they are handled, but a subsequent transfer requires *no notes anywhere on that call*. Previously written notes cannot be removed with the available tools. P8.4’s unconditional note requirement also lacks explicit exceptions for security failures and third-party calls. **Fix:** Have the memo require pending reasons to be held until a verified, non-transfer close; discard them on transfer and explicitly exempt unverified calls.

3. **MAJOR — G/cases.json, every `variables` and `products` entry.** Every tool requires `customer_ref`, but no case supplies it. Expected results reference `p1`–`p3`, yet product fixtures contain no `product_id`, and no supplied document defines automatic identifier injection. **Fix:** Supply these identifiers or document the fixture loader’s exact mapping. This is a fixture-contract defect: the supplied API otherwise has exactly the seven required paths and structurally valid OpenAPI 3.0.3 request/response definitions. [OpenAPI specification](https://spec.openapis.org/oas/v3.0.3.html)

4. **MAJOR — G/policy.md, P1.3, quoted condolence.** “I am sorry for your loss.” is copied verbatim from public Brief A, rule 9. That violates the explicit prohibition on any copied sentence, even though it is conventional language. **Fix:** Replace this sentence with independently worded condolences.

5. **MAJOR — G/policy.md, P1.2 and P6.3–P6.4; G/mail.md, complaint bullet; G/api.json, callback/transfer responses.** Callback and transfer instructions close as successful regardless of the tool result. Their APIs explicitly permit `ok: false` or HTTP 500, meaning no callback or transfer occurred. Only promise recording has a defined failure branch. **Fix:** Require successful tool results before `CB` or `XFER`; define a bounded retry and compatible failure close codes for unsuccessful actions.

6. **MINOR — G/cases.json, G-02 `script` and `expected`.** Holding the other amendments constant, refusing every proposal yields the same three `REF` notes and `NOPTP / REF` under both one-attempt and two-attempt persuasion rules. The transcript checks distinguish them, but the expected result itself does not, despite the case’s explicit discrimination intent. **Fix:** Make the customer reject the first offer but accept a second offer if incorrectly made, at least for p1; retain the one-attempt transcript check.
