## FINDINGS

1. **MAJOR — J/memo.md, §§7 and 9: failed complaint transfer after a recorded arrangement.** Section 7 preserves `arranged` unless a transfer occurs; §9 requires `no_arrangement / refused` when a complaint transfer fails twice after security. These conflict when a promise already exists and the transfer never succeeds. **Fix:** preserve `arranged` in this fallback when any promise was recorded; otherwise use `no_arrangement / refused`.

2. **MAJOR — J/policy.md, D2/E4; J/memo.md, §6: previously refused first persuasion offer.** On 21 December, a £2,000 mortgage has a 24 December deadline. If the customer proposes 29 December and rejects D2’s counteroffer of £2,000 on 24 December, E4 requires that identical offer as attempt one while also prohibiting previously refused offers. Section 6 only resolves duplication between attempts one and two. **Fix:** skip and count as used any attempt duplicating a previously refused product/amount/date combination.

3. **MAJOR — J/memo.md, §9: incomplete terminal failure handling.** `add_note` and `close_call` receive the generic retry instruction, but neither has a defined disposition after its second failure. Failed notes also leave G4’s mandatory flags unwritten, without an explicit exception. **Fix:** specify the closing result after exhausted note failures, exempt unsuccessful flags from G4’s guarantee, and define termination after an exhausted closing failure without claiming successful closure.

4. **MINOR — J/README.md, first paragraph, “recording notice required but free wording.”** G2 only restricts when recording success may be announced; it does not require an announcement. Memo §3 prohibits a replacement confirmation sentence and directs the agent to goodbye after the last recording. **Fix:** delete the README’s additional notice requirement.

5. **MINOR — J/api.json, all `200` response schemas and `/close_call` request schema.** The document is structurally valid OpenAPI 3.0.3 and contains exactly the seven required paths, but its schemas understate its contract: `{}` satisfies every success-response schema; account responses need not contain usable debts; closing requests can omit required details or supply arbitrary ones. These are contract weaknesses, not OpenAPI syntax errors. **Fix:** require `ok`, define required success payloads, constrain debts to one–three complete products, and encode the code-list result/detail combinations. [OpenAPI schema specification](https://spec.openapis.org/oas/v3.0.3.html#schema-object).
