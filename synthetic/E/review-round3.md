## FINDINGS

1. **MAJOR — E/brief.md — §10, “Exactly one `close_call`” and “Tool failures”.** The instruction to retry **any** failed tool requires a second `close_call` if the first fails, contradicting the exactly-one-call requirement. **Smallest fix:** explicitly exclude `close_call` from retries and state how an unsuccessful close is reported.

2. **MAJOR — E/brief.md — §8, “Payment plan” / “Request for a person”; §10, `promise` row and callback-failure rule.** After a recorded promise, §8 requires closing `callback_booked`, while §10 requires retaining `promise`. A twice-failed callback additionally requires `no_contact / no_callback`, contradicting the same promise rule. The general table-over-text provision does not resolve the two competing table instructions. **Smallest fix:** qualify both callback rows and the callback-failure sentence: their stated closing outcomes apply only when no promise was recorded; otherwise close `promise`.

3. **MAJOR — E/brief.md — §8, “Hardship”; E/cases.json — E-05 `expected.notes`.** Already-agreed promises must still be recorded, but the hardship definition includes products with “no promise” and no existing reason. It does not explicitly exclude agreed-but-unrecorded products. At E-05’s hardship disclosure, p1 has not been recorded, so the wording can assign it `hardship` as well as subsequently recording its promise. The expected notes assume p1 is excluded. **Smallest fix:** define hardship candidates as products with **neither an agreed nor a recorded promise**, and no existing reason.

4. **MINOR — E/brief.md — §6, “Next <weekday>”.** “Following calendar week” does not define when a week starts. On Sunday 2026-11-29, “next Monday” can consequently mean November 30 or December 7. **Smallest fix:** explicitly define a calendar week as Monday through Sunday.

5. **MINOR — E/cases.json — E-05 `checks[2]`, “the card promise of 420 dollars today is still recorded”.** A transcript can show the spoken word “today” and an absolute tool date, but cannot establish their equivalence unless `variables.now` is exposed. That transcript metadata is not guaranteed here. **Smallest fix:** require a successful `create_promise` for p1 with `amount_cents: 42000` and `pay_date: "2026-10-29"`.
