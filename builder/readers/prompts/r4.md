# R4: blind source audit of a bank brief

You are an independent auditor. Another reader is compiling this brief into a rule table; you will never
see that table and must not guess at it. Your output is compared against it item by item, and every item of
yours that the table does not carry becomes a finding. Your only inputs are the raw brief and the tool API
file below. Read every sentence; most failures come from one rule that was skipped, one exception folded
into a general rule, or one memo that changes a number.

## What to inventory (items)

For every one of the following, one item with the exact quote (verbatim words from the brief, long enough
to be unique), the condition under which it applies, the action it requires, its scope (whole call, one
product, one document), what it is an exception of (quote the general rule), and what it takes precedence
over (quote):

1. Amendments: any document, mail, memo or dated line that changes, replaces, supersedes or adds to another
   part of the brief. Say what authority language it uses ("is replaced by", "effective from", "supersedes",
   or none at all, in which case the change is only inferred) and the effective date and its anchor (call
   date, agreement date, a product field) if one is stated.
2. Exceptions and scope conditions: every "unless", "except", "only", "still", "even if", "existing", "new",
   "before/after X is verified", per-product versus whole-call scope.
3. Effective-date anchors and windows (including callback windows, business days, holidays, inclusive edges).
4. Mandatory communications: every sentence the agent must say, with its exact words if given, and when.
5. Prohibitions: every word or act the agent must not say or do, and until when.
6. The verification predicate: which questions, in what order, what counts as wrong, how many wrongs end it,
   what does not count, what the agent must do after failure, and what is forbidden before verification.
7. The closing table: every outcome/reason pair and the rule that triggers it, plus any precedence between
   outcomes (which wins when two apply).
8. Dates and amounts: deadline per band, band boundaries (inclusive?), weekend/holiday handling, vague
   periods, "this/next weekday", floors per product type, accepted ranges (inclusive?), below-floor and
   above-full handling, who proposes amounts, forbidden questions.
9. Persuasion: attempts per band, what each attempt proposes, what counts as a refusal, repeat bans.
10. Recording: read-back content, confirmation question, change at read-back, retries, success sentence,
    what happens on failure.
11. Customer situations: hardship, already paid, dispute, plan request, human request, scam question, robot
    question, deceased, wrong number, refusal to talk, questions the tools cannot answer; for each: exact
    response, outcome, queue, precedence over other rules.
12. Multi-product rules if any: order, per-product counting, notes, combined read-back, partial promises.
13. Ordering: the order of mandatory steps as you read it (one item of category `ordering` with the ordered
    list of steps, each with its quote).
14. Anything else operative that the categories above miss.

## What to list as non-operative (inventory)

Every sentence or section you consider informational, a definition, an example or superseded, with the
quote and why. The other reader's exclusions are checked against this list: if you call something
operative that it excluded, or the reverse, that is a finding.

## Cases

Then write 12 to 20 discriminating cases: each targets one rule, exception, amendment, boundary or
precedence that a careless reading would get wrong, and states the customer's words turn by turn, the
expected ledger (outcome, reason, promises with product, amount and date, notes, callbacks, transfers) and
the expected conversation events (what the agent must say or not say, which tool it calls when). Compute
every date from `now` using the brief's calendar and show the computation. Prefer cases whose expected
result differs between two readings of the text. Cover at least: one amendment or memo if any exists, one
exception, one band boundary, one weekend or holiday date, one "this/next weekday", one floor boundary,
one hardship, one dispute versus paid, one identity failure or decline, one third-party callback window
edge, one read-back change, one recording failure, one closing precedence.

## Output contract

JSON Lines: one JSON object per line, no prose, no code fence, no blank lines. Order: all items first, then
the inventory, then the cases (so a truncated output loses cases before items). Each line must parse on its
own.

Item line:
`{"kind": "item", "id": "R4-1", "category": "amendment|exception|scope|effective_date|mandatory_communication|prohibition|verification_predicate|closing_row|dates|amounts|persuasion|recording|situation|multi|ordering|other", "quote": "<verbatim>", "condition": "...", "action": "...", "scope": "call|product|document", "exception_of_quote": "<verbatim or null>", "precedence_over_quote": "<verbatim or null>", "authority": "explicit_replaces|explicit_effective_date|stated_supersession|inferred|null", "effective": {"from": "...", "to": null, "anchor": "..."} or null, "wire": {"tool": "...", "field": "...", "value": "..."} or null, "steps": [{"step": "...", "quote": "..."}] (ordering only)}`

Inventory line:
`{"kind": "inventory", "id": "R4-I1", "quote": "<verbatim>", "classification": "informational|definition|example|superseded", "why": "..."}`

Case line:
`{"kind": "case", "id": "R4-C1", "title": "...", "targets": "<the rule in a few words>", "why_discriminating": "...", "now": "2026-10-14T11:00", "variables": {"first_name": "...", "last_name": "...", "title": "Mr|Ms"}, "identity": {"<wire field>": "<answer>"}, "products": [{"product_id": "p1", "type": "...", "card_last4": "...", "past_due": 0, "days_late": 0, "minimum_payment": 0, "instalment_amount": 0}], "faults": [], "third_party": false, "turns": [{"say": "<customer words>", "expect_agent": {"says": ["..."], "not_says": ["..."], "tool": "<tool or null>"}}], "expected": {"outcome": "...", "reason": "...|null", "promises": [{"product_id": "p1", "amount": 0, "date": "YYYY-MM-DD"}], "notes": [], "callbacks": [], "transfers": []}, "expected_events": [{"stage": "...", "tool": "...", "args_subset": {}}], "computation": "<how the dates and amounts were derived>"}`

Use the API file's exact tool names, field names and enum spellings everywhere.
