## FINDINGS

1. **MAJOR — C/brief.md — rules 3, 32, 39–42 and 46; precedence clause in rule 16.** Some simultaneous triggers require incompatible actions. “Can I speak to a person?” during name confirmation requires another identity-confirmation question under rule 3 but immediate transfer without questions under rule 42. “I was hospitalized; I have to go, bye” while a question is open requires `handed_over/welfare` under rule 32 and `no_pledge/refused` under rule 46. “I already paid; get me a person” triggers both settlement and transfer. The existing precedence clause does not resolve these combinations. **Smallest fix:** extend the precedence clause to cover these terminal triggers and explicitly exempt them from rule 3’s repeat question.

2. **MAJOR — C/brief.md — rules 39, 40 and 45 versus rule 47’s `pledge` outcome.** After a successful pledge, a payment claim, dispute or repeated refusal to talk can require `settled` or `no_pledge`, while rule 47 also assigns `pledge` because a pledge exists. Rules 16 and 46 explicitly preserve the pledge outcome; these other rules do not specify their interaction with it. **Smallest fix:** state the outcome precedence after recording, and qualify rules 39, 40 and 45 accordingly.

3. **MAJOR — C/brief.md — rules 12–17 and rule 47’s “Tool failures”; C/api.json — `/verify_identity` and `/get_account`, responses `200` and `500`.** Both tools expose operational failures, but the brief provides no bounded recovery or terminal outcome for them. An identity-service error is not necessarily a wrong answer, and an account-service error supplies no balance to state. **Smallest fix:** specify a retry limit, distinguish service errors from incorrect answers, and assign an existing terminal outcome/reason after exhausted retries, without disclosing unavailable account information.

4. **MINOR — C/brief.md — rules 20–24 and 30.** A past business date is prohibited by rule 20, but no rule explains how to handle it. Rule 24 handles only dates beyond the deadline, while rule 30 prohibits treating a proposed date as refusal. A past weekend date can also roll forward under rule 21 to a date still before today. **Smallest fix:** add an explicit past-date branch that offers the earliest permissible business date and requires confirmation.

5. **MINOR — C/brief.md — rule 16’s opening instructions versus rule 47’s final sentence.** Rule 16 places telling the member that the request “is noted” before calling `add_note`; rule 47 prohibits claiming it was noted before success. This becomes misleading if both attempts fail. **Smallest fix:** explicitly place the acknowledgment after `ok: true`; specify neutral wording if both attempts fail.
